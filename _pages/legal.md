## 3. Cookies and Consent Management

We use **consentmanager**, provided by consentmanager AB, Sweden, to manage visitor preferences for cookies and optional tracking technologies.

Cookies and similar browser technologies may be used to remember privacy preferences, maintain security, and support optional website functionality.

### 3.1 Essential Cookies

Essential cookies support functions such as security protection and consent preference management.

Examples observed on the website include:

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare | Records successful completion of certain security challenges |
| `_cmpcc` | consentmanager | Consent management |
| `cmpcccu77286` | consentmanager | Consent preference management |
| `cmpiuid` | consentmanager | Consent-related identifier |
| `__cmpiuid` | consentmanager | Consent-related identifier |

These cookie names were observed during testing. Their classification, exact purpose, and lifetime depend on the relevant provider configuration.

Essential cookies do not require consent where an applicable legal exemption permits their use.

### 3.2 Optional Analytics Cookies

Where Google Analytics is enabled, it may use cookies such as:

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes browser instances for analytics |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Maintains analytics session-related state |

These are potential analytics cookies. Their inclusion does not indicate that they are currently being placed by the website.

Optional analytics cookies and tracking technologies requiring consent are intended to remain disabled unless you grant consent.

### 3.3 Managing Cookie Preferences

You can accept, reject, or modify optional cookie preferences using the consent management interface.

You may withdraw consent at any time through the **Cookie Settings** control on the website.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Deleting cookies may reset stored consent preferences.
